Joint Statement

A Decade of Institutional Maturity: From Sovereign Issuer to Vibrant Secondary Market

"As Angola accelerates its economic diversification away from crude dependency, capital markets have transitioned from an emerging debt venue into a primary engine for corporate capital formation."

Over the past 36 months, the Angolan capital market landscape has undergone an extraordinary structural metamorphosis. Under the combined stewardship of BODIVA and leading financial intermediaries like BFA Capital Markets, secondary market liquidity has expanded beyond institutional bank balances to encompass pension funds, private wealth, and corporate treasuries.

The ongoing execution of the State Privatization Program (PROPRIV) marks a definitive paradigm shift. With successful equity listings of commercial banking anchors such as Banco Angolano de Investimentos (BAI) and Banco Caixa Geral Angola (BCGA), investor participation has expanded exponentialy. The market is no longer solely focused on sovereign debt issuances; it now encompasses dividend-yielding equity securities and liquidity-generating repo agreements.

Strategic Imperatives for 2026/2027

Our joint advisory Framework focuses on three pillars: (1) Extending sovereign debt maturity profiles to reduce short-term refinancing risks, (2) Deepening secondary equity market liquidity via market-maker frameworks, and (3) Integrating regional SADC clearing mechanisms to attract international ESG capital.

Macro Analysis

The Kwanza, Inflation, and Sovereign Yield Curves: Anchoring Stability

Monetary policy adjustments by the Banco Nacional de Angola (BNA) have effectively anchored foreign exchange volatility, opening a strategic window for long-duration fixed-income allocations.

Angola's macroeconomic architecture in 2026 demonstrates remarkable resilience against global commodity headwinds. Stable oil export receipts alongside conservative fiscal management have allowed the Ministry of Finance to rationalize its issuance calendar, prioritizing Treasury Bonds indexed to foreign currencies (OT-TX) alongside high-yielding non-readjustable bonds (OT-NR).

Yield curves have begun displaying a healthier downward-sloping structure at the long end, signaling market confidence in medium-term disinflation. Institutional managers are advised to lock in double-digit yields on 5-to-7 year OT-NR instruments before expected rate cuts materialize in early 2027.

SECTION 03 • EQUITY MARKETS PROPRIV PRIVATIZATION PIPELINE

Equity Growth Drivers

From State Ownership to Public Floating: Catalytic IPOs

The rollout of Angola's Privatization Program (PROPRIV) is creating a core universe of dividend-rich equities, transforming BODIVA into an active venue for retail and foreign capital.

Following the landmark listings of BAI and Caixa Geral Angola, the market capitalization of BODIVA's equity segment has expanded dramatically. The presence of regulated, high-performing financial institutions has established liquid benchmarks, encouraging broader corporate issuers to explore initial public offerings.

The prospective floating of state insurance titan ENSA Seguros alongside non-energy assets from Sonangol represents the next growth catalyst. Institutional managers at BFA note that early equity listings have consistently delivered robust dividend yields exceeding 12-14%, offering exceptional real-rate protection against regional inflation.

SECTION 04 • SECONDARY DEBT & REPOS LIQUIDITY MECHANISMS

Fixed Income Operations

Secondary Debt Trading & Repo Operations: Expanding Institutional Depth

The standardization of repurchase agreements (Repos) on BODIVA's platform has transformed liquidity management for commercial banks and corporate treasuries across Angola.

Historically dominated by buy-and-hold sovereign paper, BODIVA's secondary market now records consistent daily trading volumes. The introduction of automated Repo matching mechanisms allows institutional investors to monetize long-term sovereign bond holdings without triggering outright sales, unlocking short-term working capital.

Furthermore, corporate bond issuances are gaining traction. Private sector champions within manufacturing, telecom, and logistics are utilizing BODIVA-listed debt to bypass traditional bank loan constraints, securing fixed rates for 3-to-5 year expansion project financing.

SECTION 05 • FUTURE HORIZONS ESG & REGIONAL INTEGRATION

Green Finance & Regional Growth

ESG Frameworks & SADC Capital Integration

Connecting Angola’s financial ecosystem with regional SADC debt markets and global green capital pools.

As sustainable finance transitions from niche concept to mainstream imperative, BODIVA is establishing regulatory frameworks for Green, Social, and Sustainability (GSS) bonds. These instruments aim to finance crucial renewable energy projects, water sanitation systems, and sustainable agricultural developments across Angola's provinces.

Simultaneously, integration efforts via the Committee of SADC Stock Exchanges (CoSSE) are advancing cross-border dual listings. This allows international asset managers operating in Johannesburg or London to trade Angolan securities seamlessly through standardized clearing protocols.
